UNDERSTANDING PANCHMAHABHUT

Five great elements.
One natural world.

In Ayurveda, the natural world is understood through five fundamental elements known as Panchmahabhut. They are Prithvi, Jal, Tej, Vayu and Akash — commonly understood as Earth, Water, Fire, Air and Space.

These five ideas give us a way to look at nature through qualities such as solidity, fluidity, transformation, movement and space.
